ARTIST STATEMENT:\
My work shows the randomization of two overlapping sets of shapes: linear lines and circles. Every integration of linear lines is set to be at random set areas of the window making a distinct illusionistic overlap. Every integration of circles is set at specific points outside of the area, creating randomized distinct folds. I first focused on lines to mimic how computer screens interact with the vision of the viewer. So that each line is randomized in thickness, transpareceny and direction (with the help of rotation). Likewise reintegrating some aspects into the next sets of circles sections. Only difference is the set anchor point. \
You\'92ve ever accidentally drop a liquid to a screen and notice the magnified truth of colors? I wanted to bring it to you. I wanted the viewer to truly see how colors do interact with one another, compiled in interesting ways, to appreciate the illusion of colors to make one. }
